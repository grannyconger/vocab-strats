Picture It

Words become images and maps. First exposure. Mental hooks.

Make It Visual

Draw it. Own it.

Best for
New listsQuiet workLow prep
Materials
8-box handout. One word per box.
  1. Pass out the sheet.
  2. One drawing per word.
  3. Personal meaning. Links to something they know.

Coach itWeird beats pretty. Inside jokes stick.

Concept Map

One hard word. Total deep dive.

Best for
Abstract wordsRepeat misses
Materials
Paper or digital template.
  1. Word in the center.
  2. Branch out:
    • Definition. Part of speech.
    • Synonyms. Antonyms.
    • Word origin.
    • Visual.
    • Used in a sentence.
    • What it is NOT.
  3. Add anything that helps a peer.

Coach it1 to 3 tough words. Not the whole list.

Connection Map

Link every word. Label every link.

Best for
ReviewHigher-orderBig picture
Materials
Large white paper. Markers.
  1. Write one word.
  2. Add a connected word. Draw a line.
  3. On the line: HOW they connect. Words or sketch.
  4. Repeat. Link to any word on the page. Use them all.

Coach itThe line is the learning. Ban "they're related."

Think It Through

Reason it out. No lookups. Critical thinking.

Is and Is Not

Original ideas only. No dictionary answers.

Best for
Higher-orderAbstract wordsExposing shallow recall
Materials
Fill-in frame per word.
  1. Frame: "[Word] is ___. [Word] is not ___."
  2. Banned: definitions, synonyms, antonyms.
  3. Invent concepts, examples, scenarios.

Coach itGoogleable? Doesn't count. That rule forces thinking.

Context Clue Sentences

Write so a stranger could crack it.

Best for
ComprehensionDefinition to usageSolo work
Materials
Word list. Clue-type chart.
  1. One sentence per word.
  2. Each uses one clue type:
ClueMove
DefinitionStates the meaning.
InferenceEnough detail to deduce it.
ExampleShows an instance.
SynonymPairs with a known twin.
AntonymContrasts with its opposite.

Coach itSwap. Blank the word. Partner guesses. Clues worked?

Write It

Words at work. For "knows it, never uses it."

Free Write

Any topic. Max vocab.

Best for
Real-writing transferZero prepWarm-upExit task
Materials
Paper or doc. Word list.
  1. Student picks the topic.
  2. Confident words first.
  3. Then notes. Work in the shaky ones.

Coach itHighlight each vocab word. Fast scan for usage.

Shared Silly Stories

Pass the paper. One word per sentence.

Best for
CollaborationFun reviewWords in context
Materials
Sheet per student. Word list stapled on. Circle seating.
  1. Write an opening sentence. Use one word.
  2. Cross it off.
  3. Pass right.
  4. Read. Add a sentence. New word. Cross it off.
  5. Repeat until every word is used.

Coach itRead a few aloud. Silly hooks. Misuse sparks talk.

Teach and Present

Students as experts. Slides for peers. Devices needed.

Teach a Word

One student. One word. One slide. Teach it.

Best for
New listsShared referenceSpeaking practice
Materials
Google Slide each. Shared deck to collect.
  1. Assign one word each.
  2. Slide includes:
    • Word. Definition.
    • Part of speech. Origin.
    • Synonyms. Antonyms.
    • Visual.
    • Sentence.
    • Anything helpful.
  3. Short presentation. Teach the class.
  4. All slides into one shared doc.

Coach itInstant study guide. Post it in your LMS (Learning Management System, like Google Classroom).

Guess a Word

Clue slides. Peers guess.

Best for
Quiz reviewSocial energyCreativity
Materials
Google Slide each. Combined deck.
  1. Assign one word each.
  2. Build a slide. Related words, phrases, images.
  3. No definitions. Connections only.
  4. Combine into one deck.
  5. Guess from peer clues.

Coach itSmall groups. More turns. More guessing.

Act It Out

Move. Speak. Perform. Low-energy days. Kinesthetic learners.

Charades

Silent acting. Class guesses.

Best for
Restless classQuick reviewNear-zero prep
Materials
Words on slips or cards.
  1. Draw a word.
  2. Act it out. No talking.
  3. Class guesses. Rotate actors.

Coach itAbstract words are hard to act. Good. Forces real meaning.

Vocabulary Improv

Random word. Into the skit.

Best for
SpeakingNatural usageHigh energy
Materials
Container of word slips per group.
  1. Groups. One container each.
  2. Take turns drawing a word.
  3. Work it into the improv scene.

Coach itGive a starting scene. Job interview. Lost on vacation. Focus stays on words.

Reinforce Everywhere

Beyond your room. Long-term stickiness.

Enlist Other Teachers

Colleagues drop the words. Students race to spot them.

Best for
RetentionCross-curricularBetter listening
Materials
Short email to same-grade colleagues.
  1. Share your list.
  2. Willing colleagues use a few words that week.
  3. Students listen in other classes.
  4. First to hear it wins.

Coach itUnexpected place. Memorable word. Keep it small: 2 to 3 words per colleague per week.
